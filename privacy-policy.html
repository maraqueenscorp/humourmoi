Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Humour Moi is a meme-browsing app. You can open the app and read memes without creating an account or providing your name, email address, or phone number.

This Privacy Policy explains how Maraqueens Corperation ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Humour Moi mobile application ("the App"). We aim to collect as little information as possible while supporting the App and showing advertisements.

1. Information You Provide

Humour Moi does not require registration or sign-in. We do not ask you to submit your name, email address, phone number, or account password to browse memes.

We do not operate a user profile or account system in the App. If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

Humour Moi uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google. Its software development kit may automatically collect and process certain information from your device and your use of the App to deliver, measure, personalise (where applicable), and protect advertising services.

Depending on the SDK version, configuration, your device settings, and applicable consent choices, this information may include:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Advertising ID or other device/app identifiers.
  • Ad views, taps, and other interactions with the App or advertisements.
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as app launch or crash information.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. The information is handled by Google under its own privacy terms. We do not control Google's independent data practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob data disclosure for Android.

3. Sharing Memes

When you tap the share button, the App opens your device's standard sharing menu so you can choose another app or platform. You decide which platform to use and what to do next.

Humour Moi does not itself publish content to your social accounts or ask you for your social media login details. Any information you choose to share is handled by the platform you select, according to that platform's own terms and privacy policy. Please review the selected platform's privacy practices before sharing.

4. How We Use Information

We use information we receive directly from you (for example, an email you send us) to respond to your enquiries and provide support. Information processed by AdMob is used by Google and its advertising partners for the purposes described above.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

We do not operate a Humour Moi user-account database and do not collect registration details through the App. We retain correspondence you send us only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, provide support, and keep appropriate business records.

Information collected by Google AdMob is subject to Google's systems, retention practices, and security measures. Data handling can vary according to Google's services and your settings. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the security of information handled by third-party services.

6. Children's Privacy

Humour Moi is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because advertisements may be provided by a third-party SDK, the advertising configuration and any age-related settings must be selected and maintained in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play and AdMob requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us.

7. Your Choices and Controls

  • You can choose whether to use the share feature and which platform to open.
  • You can manage or reset your Android advertising ID through your device's settings, where those controls are available.
  • You can manage ad personalisation and privacy choices through Google's available settings and consent controls, where applicable.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

The App uses Google AdMob and may open third-party apps when you choose to share a meme. Those services are operated independently and have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to read their policies because this Privacy Policy does not govern their practices.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, advertising services, legal requirements, or our practices. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Please review this page periodically.
